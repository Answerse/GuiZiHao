DISCOVER CHENPI

What Is Chenpi?

Chenpi is the dried peel of mature mandarins that has been carefully harvested, sun-dried, and naturally aged over time. As it matures, its aroma, color, and flavor continue to evolve, making it a unique product shaped by the value of time.

WAYS TO ENJOY

How to Enjoy Chenpi

Brew as Tea

Steep aged Chenpi on its own or blend it with your favorite tea for a rich citrus aroma and smooth finish.

Cook with Flavor

Add Chenpi to soups, stews, and braised dishes to enhance aroma and depth of flavor.

Bake & Create

Use Chenpi in desserts, pastries, or creative recipes to add natural citrus notes.
